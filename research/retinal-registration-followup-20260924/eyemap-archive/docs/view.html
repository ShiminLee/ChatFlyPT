Interactive viewer

Points are colored by hex coordinate v = p + q. Hover a point to see its p, q, theta, phi (and rootid where available). Mollweide/Mercator are computed client-side from each point's x, y, z viewing-direction vector — the Mollweide projection is a direct port of the same math used in connectome_interpreter's plot_mollweide_projection.
